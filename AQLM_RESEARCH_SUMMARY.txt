IMPORTANT NOTES
================================================================================

1. Pre-quantized models RECOMMENDED over custom quantization
   - Quantization process is computationally expensive (hours on H100)
   - Pre-quantized: 20+ models ready to use
   - Custom quantization: Only if unique model or specialized bit-width

2. Windows compatibility notes
   - Triton dependency may cause issues on Windows
   - Workaround: Use WSL2 or Docker
   - Triton not required for 1x16 and 2x8 configs

3. llama.cpp & Ollama incompatibility is PERMANENT
   - Architectural difference between AQLM and GGUF quantization
   - No planned support in llama.cpp roadmap
   - Community consensus: Use vLLM for AQLM instead

4. Quality-performance trade-off
   - 2-bit AQLM: Sweet spot (6-8x compression, 95%+ quality)
   - 1-bit AQLM: Extreme compression (10-15x), 80-90% quality on smaller models
   - Larger models (70B) retain better quality at extreme compression

5. Latest developments (April 2025)
   - AQLM v1.1.7 released with 8D codebook support
   - vLLM official partnership announced
   - 1-bit model quality improvements (7.85 PPL)
   - HuggingFace Hub v4.47+ has enhanced AQLM support
